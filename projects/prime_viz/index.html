4D Prime Spiral Data Structure

Purpose: This visualization maps prime numbers into a 4-dimensional geometric space, revealing patterns and relationships that are not visible in traditional representations.

Key Concepts:

  • Spatial Encoding: Each digit of a prime number is encoded as a point in 4D space
  • Pattern Recognition: Similar primes create similar geometric structures
  • Dimensional Analysis: The 4th dimension (W) encodes additional mathematical properties

Potential Applications:

  • Cryptography: Novel approaches to prime-based cryptographic systems
  • Number Theory: Visual exploration of prime patterns and relationships
  • Quantum Computing: Basis for multi-dimensional quantum states encoding
  • Data Compression: Geometric encoding for efficient prime representation
  • AI Pattern Recognition: Training neural networks on geometric prime patterns

Mathematical Formulation:

For each digit position (i) in the prime number:

x = R * cos(2πi/P)
y = R * sin(2πi/P)
z = (h/P) * i
w = A * sin(digit * π/10)

Where:

  • R = radius of the spiral
  • P = period for X and Y rotation
  • h = height increment
  • A = amplitude of the 4th dimension
  • digit = the actual digit value at position i

Future Extensions: This approach can be extended to even higher dimensions, creating a complete geometric algebra of prime numbers.
