About Dyadic Maps

A dyadic map takes a number between 0 and 1, multiplies it by another number, and keeps only the fractional part (mod 1).

When using prime numbers as multipliers, these maps exhibit quantum-like randomness properties.

Try different values, especially large prime numbers, and observe how they affect the distribution of dice rolls!
